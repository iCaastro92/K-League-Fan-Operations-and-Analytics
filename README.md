## 📌 Executive Summary & Business Problem
In the highly competitive sports entertainment market, acquiring a new fan is significantly more expensive than retaining an existing one. The K-League faces a challenge with fan churn, particularly among users on free tiers who fail to convert to paid memberships or merchandise buyers. 

This project is an end-to-end **Predictive CRM pipeline** designed to identify at-risk fans using Machine Learning and automatically trigger targeted retention campaigns. By moving from a reactive to a proactive strategy, the club can optimize marketing spend and protect baseline revenue.
